05 · The diagnosis

Where does delivery
actually break?

Claude-Opus-5.5 has the highest F2P score, so its recorded trajectories become a microscope. The traces connect each failed test to its requirement, the colleague who holds it, the question, the patch, and the final handoff note. The agent finds the right people. It loses track of individual requirements afterward.

THE TAKEAWAY

Asking is not acquiring.
Acquiring is not verifying.
Shipping is not finishing.

Dependable delivery means tracking every requirement until it is closed: raise it with its owner, revisit deferred questions, implement it, verify it against the tests, and report its true status in the handoff.
